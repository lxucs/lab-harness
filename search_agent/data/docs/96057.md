---
title: Model Devon Windsor and Husband Johnny Barbara Welcome Second Child Together
date: 2023-05-04
---
Model Devon Windsor is a mom of two! The former Victoria's Secret model and her husband Johnny Barbara, who revealed their pregnancy last November, have announced the arrival of their second child together. Their little one was born on May 1st.

To announce the news, Devon shared a picture of herself holding her newborn in the hospital. The couple welcomed a baby girl and chose to name her Celine Blue Barbara. Celine is the couple's second daughter. The two welcomed their first daughter, Enzo Elodie, in 2021. Last month, the swimsuit designer shared a picture of her holding Enzo with the caption, "My little love, can't wait to see you become a big sister!"

Devon and Johnny first met through mutual friends in New York City but both were in relationships at the time. Two years later, the couple crossed paths in Miami in 2016. Devon made the first move by getting Johnny's number and inviting him out for dinner. In 2018, Johnny proposed to Devon in an elaborate beach engagement. He had the words "Marry Me?" written in the sand. Their proposal was followed by an extravagant three-day wedding celebration in St. Barths in 2019, with the model wearing nine bridal looks throughout.

In March, the couple took a babymoon vacation to Florida ahead of Celine's arrival. Devon shared a snap of her and her husband and wrote, "Excited to kick back and really take this time to relax before we become a family of 4! Can't believe this is the first trip Johnny and I have been on alone since our last baby moon before Zo! Time flies, soaking in all these moments!"

Congratulations to Devon and Johnny on the arrival of their second daughter! We hope they enjoy their lives as parents of two.
