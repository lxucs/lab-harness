title: Brenda Song Reveals Name of Her and Macaulay Culkin's 2-Year-Old Son
author: Tionah Lee
date: 2025-01-07
---
Brenda Song is giving new insight into her and Macaulay Culkin's family.

Including sharing the name of their youngest son for the first time. More than two years after the pair quietly welcomed their son in March 2022, the Suite Life of Zach & Cody star revealed to Bustle in a new interview that their 2-year-old's son's name is Carson.

Fans got the first look at Brenda, 36, and Macaulay's children—including their son Dakota, 3—when the boys appeared alongside their parents during the Home Alone star's Hollywood Walk of Fame ceremony in 2023.

And it was a public outing the notoriously private couple—who got engaged in 2022—couldn't let their boys miss.

"That was such a special moment," Brenda told Bustle. "We couldn't have them miss that. Dak was so scared of all the cameras. That's one of the reasons why we sort of keep them away [from the spotlight]. They did not understand what was happening."

Still, Brenda would rather keep her children out of the public eye, as they are starting to notice when their privacy is being invaded.

"My son was like, 'Why is that man taking pictures of you, mama?'" the Dollface actress noted about a run-in with paparazzi at Dakota's soccer practice. "You could take pictures of me all day. I don't care. But when it's your kids, it's different. They didn't ask for this life."

And while she has found someone who is equally as reserved as she is, the Disney alum admitted that her boys are pretty busy.

"Mack and I are both very antisocial," she said of her 44-year-old fiancé. "Our kids have more of a social calendar than I will ever have."

However, as of late, Brenda's been juggling a busy schedule. And when it came to new movie The Last Showgirl, which she appears in opposite Pamela Anderson and Jamie Lee Curtis, she admitted she was hesitant to take on the role as she navigated life as a mother and working actress.

"I had two pregnancies in the same calendar year. It was a lot," Brenda told Bustle. "Once I stepped into motherhood, it made me question a lot: my place in this industry, my place as a woman, ageism in this industry, society's pressures of what beauty is, what being young is. Things that I'd never questioned before."

"Family life is the most important thing," she added, "but I've always said to be the best parent, the best partner, I have to be the best me. And a huge part of that is work. I have to be able to honor that."
